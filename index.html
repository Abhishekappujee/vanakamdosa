Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a long-term contract? keyboard_arrow_down

No, we believe in freedom. You can choose monthly plans and cancel anytime with a 30-day notice. We also offer discounted annual plans for committed members.

Can I freeze my membership if I travel? keyboard_arrow_down

Yes! Annual members can freeze their membership for up to 45 days per year. Monthly members can pause for a small maintenance fee.

Do you offer a free trial? keyboard_arrow_down

Absolutely. We offer a complimentary 1-Day Pass so you can experience our facilities, classes, and vibe before committing.

Are personal trainers included in the membership? keyboard_arrow_down

General floor trainers are always available to help with safety and basic guidance. Dedicated 1-on-1 Personal Training is an add-on service customized to your goals.

What are the gym timings? keyboard_arrow_down

We are open 24/7 for members with key-fob access. Staffed hours are from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily.
